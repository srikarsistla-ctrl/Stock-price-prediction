Why this page exists. A backtest can always be tuned until it looks good. A forward log cannot — the prediction is written to SQLite before it is rendered, keyed by session date so a page refresh cannot create a second attempt, and the outcome is filled in from real market data. If the model is worse than the backtest claims, this table is where it shows up.
